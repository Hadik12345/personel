"AlphaJavis" is an agentic next-generation voice assistant,
built to handle everything from device control, music playback, research, and automation while understanding your context and emotions.
It features ambient sound awareness, intent detection, and wake-word recognition (“Javis”), supporting 25 languages across PC / mobile.
AlphaJavis delivers powerful actions, from simple reminders to complex tasks like purchasing products on your command.

"AlphaJavis" goes beyond traditional assistants and enables rich, human-like interaction. From emotional awareness, ambient listening, and intelligent intent recognition to full device control, every element is designed to feel natural. You can automate tasks, generate summaries, search deeply, or issue complex multi-step commands—all handled smoothly by AlphaJavis, bringing new possibilities to voice-based experiences.

01

Advanced Emotional & Environmental Awareness

Create a new digital space

AlphaJavis understands emotional tone and ambient sound in real time. It listens intelligently to your surroundings, adapts to your mood, and delivers responses that feel natural and human-like.

AlphaJavis uses emotional understanding and ambient sound detection to create a deeply adaptive voice experience. It senses how you feel, reacts accordingly, and becomes more intuitive over time.

02

Precise Wake-Word & Intent Recognition

Freedom of expression

AlphaJavis instantly responds to wake-words like “Javis” and understands the intention behind your requests. It listens more like a human by decoding context, goals, and meaning—not just commands.

With powerful intent recognition and fast wake-word detection, AlphaJavis understands what you mean, not just what you say. It reacts quickly and smartly, making interactions smooth and natural.

03

Powerful Agentic Automation

Fusion of Technology and Expression

AlphaJavis performs both simple and multi-step complex tasks—setting reminders, managing apps, searching the web, generating summaries, and even executing actions like purchasing items—all on your behalf.

AlphaJavis goes beyond basic commands with true agentic abilities. It can automate workflows, manage apps, complete requests, and carry out complex tasks end-to-end, acting like a real digital assistant.

CASE 01

WEAR GO LAND

丸紅株式会社様と共同で、デジタル体験構築基盤「stellla」を活用したメタバースファッションモール「Wear Go Land」を企画・開発。幅広い服の着せ替え、カスタマイズ機能を採用し、スマートフォンの参加できる新しいデジタルファッションモール体験を実現しました。

CASE 02

DISCOAT 2025SS EXHIBITION in virtual

東芝テック株式会社様と共同で、デジタル体験構築基盤「stellla」を活用した没入型バーチャルファッションショー「DISCOAT 2025SS EXHIBITION in virtual」を企画・開発。Unreal Engineの高品質レンダリングとクラウドサービスを組み合わせ、スマートフォンのブラウザから参加できる新しいデジタルファッション体験を実現しました。

CASE 03

荏原メタバースインターンシップ2023夏

荏原製作所は、没入型デジタル体験構築基盤「stellla」を活用し、「荏原メタバースインターンシップ2023夏」を実施。stelllaのカスタマイズ性を活かし、荏原製作所のブランドイメージに沿った独自のデジタル空間を構築しました。
