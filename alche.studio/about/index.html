About me

Hi, I'm Hardik Singh. From the classrooms
and labs of Delhi Public School, Kalyanpur,
I'm on a mission to explore, create, and innovate.

My world revolves around the thrill of discovery –
whether that's unraveling the complexities of AI , transforming software concepts into working hardware like my voice assistant,
or capturing life's dynamism through my award-winning photography.
Being an active member of Hack Club has fueled my passion for collaborative coding,

while leading creative teams and mentoring juniors has taught me the power of shared vision. I believe in the power of a well-researched idea, a thoughtfully crafted line of code, and a compellingly told story.

This space showcases some of the projects I'm most proud of.
I hope they give you a glimpse into my journey and my excitement for what's next. Feel free to reach out!
